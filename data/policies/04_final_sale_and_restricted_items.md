# NovaMart Final Sale and Restricted Items Policy

- **Policy ID:** POL-RES-004
- **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15
- **Owner:** NovaMart Operations — Customer Policy Office
- **Summary:** Lists merchandise that is strictly non-returnable (Final Sale/deep clearance, gift cards, activated software, opened hygiene items) and defines the single exception for items damaged in transit.

## 1. Scope
1.1. This policy overrides `POL-RET-001` and `POL-ELC-002` for every item listed in Section 2.
1.2. Associates must check this policy before accepting any return of clearance, gift card, software, or personal hygiene merchandise.

## 2. Strictly Non-Returnable Items
The following items are **non-returnable and non-refundable**:
2.1. **Clearance merchandise** marked **Final Sale**, or discounted by **50% or more** from its original price.
   - Example: `PROD-606` — Nordic Winter Parka (Clearance - 60% Off), $64.00 (original $160.00), category `Apparel (Final Sale)`.
2.2. **Gift Cards**, both physical and digital.
2.3. **Activated digital software** and downloadable license keys.
2.4. **Opened personal hygiene items**, including **In-Ear Headphones/Earbuds**.
   - Example: `PROD-505` — SonicPulse Pro In-Ear Wireless Earbuds, $160.00, category `Audio / Personal Care`.
   - Unopened `PROD-505` units with an intact seal may be returned within the standard 30-day window in `POL-RET-001`.

## 3. Defective on Arrival (Hygiene Items)
3.1. An opened `PROD-505` may be accepted only if it is **defective on arrival** (for example, one earbud does not power on or will not pair).
3.2. The defect must be reported within **7 calendar days** of delivery or purchase.
3.3. The associate verifies the defect at the counter and processes an identical exchange in-store. Opened earbuds are never resold.

## 4. Single Exception: Damaged in Transit
4.1. If a restricted or Final Sale item from an **online order** arrives **physically damaged in transit**, it is eligible for:
   - an **identical replacement**, or
   - a **refund** to the original payment method if the item is out of stock.
4.2. The damage must be reported within **7 calendar days of delivery**. Reports on Day 8 or later are declined.
4.3. The value threshold in `POL-ORD-003` still applies:
   - `PROD-606` ($64.00, <= $150.00): resolve at the register.
   - `PROD-505` ($160.00, > $150.00): create an Operations Escalation Ticket with photos and the `order_id`.
4.4. This exception does not apply to gift cards or activated digital software, which have no physical transit damage.

## 5. Associate Guidance
5.1. Change of mind, wrong size, or "no longer needed" requests for items in Section 2 must be politely declined.
5.2. Denials clearly covered by this policy are handled in-store and are not escalated (see `SOP-OPS-007`).
5.3. Restricted items are also excluded from competitor price matching (see `POL-PRC-005`).
