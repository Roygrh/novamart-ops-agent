## 2. PROD-101 — BrewMaster Pro Espresso Machine
2.1. Standard 30-day return under `POL-RET-001`.
2.2. Confirm the water tank, portafilter, and steam wand are included before accepting a return.

## 3. PROD-202 — NovaAir FPV Drone 4K
3.1. 15-day return window and 15% open-box restocking fee under `POL-ELC-002`.
3.2. Contains a **Lithium-Polymer (LiPo) battery**.
3.3. **Damaged units cannot be shipped via standard air freight.** Any damaged, swollen, or punctured unit must be returned using a **ground hazmat return label** only.
3.4. Store damaged units in the designated battery-safe bin; never place them in general returns cages.
3.5. Transit damage on this item ($450.00 > $150.00) requires an Operations Escalation Ticket (`POL-ORD-003`).

## 4. PROD-303 — VisionMax 65" 4K OLED Smart TV
4.1. 15-day return window and 15% open-box restocking fee under `POL-ELC-002`.
4.2. **Mandatory serial number verification:** before accepting any return, staff must confirm that the serial number on the **TV chassis** matches the serial number on the **box** and on the **receipt**.
4.3. If any of the three serial numbers do not match, do NOT accept the return. Create an Operations Escalation Ticket (`SOP-OPS-007`).
4.4. Inspect the panel for cracks or pressure marks with the TV upright; never lay the TV flat.

## 5. PROD-404 — Heritage Pre-Seasoned Cast Iron Cocotte
5.1. Standard 30-day return under `POL-RET-001`.
5.2. **Lifetime Cast Iron Replacement Warranty:** covers **structural cracks** that occur under **normal cooking use**, at any time after purchase.
5.3. The warranty is **handled directly in-store**; no escalation is required. Verify the crack, then process an identical replacement at the POS using "Warranty Replacement".
5.4. Surface rust, seasoning wear, and damage from dropping or misuse are not structural cracks under normal use.

## 6. PROD-505 — SonicPulse Pro In-Ear Wireless Earbuds
6.1. Restricted hygiene item: **non-returnable once opened**, unless defective on arrival (`POL-RES-004`).
6.2. Check the hygiene seal; an opened seal means the item cannot be returned for change of mind.

## 7. PROD-606 — Nordic Winter Parka (Clearance - 60% Off)
7.1. Marked **Final Sale**: non-returnable and non-refundable unless damaged in transit (`POL-RES-004`).
7.2. Excluded from competitor price matching (`POL-PRC-005`).
