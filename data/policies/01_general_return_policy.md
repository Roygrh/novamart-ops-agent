# NovaMart General Return Policy

- **Policy ID:** POL-RET-001
- **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15
- **Owner:** NovaMart Operations — Customer Policy Office
- **Summary:** Defines the standard 30-calendar-day return window, item condition requirements, and refund rules (with and without a receipt) for Home & Kitchen, Apparel, and general merchandise.

## 1. Scope
1.1. This policy applies to all `Home & Kitchen`, `Apparel`, and general merchandise sold in NovaMart stores or online.
1.2. Covered products include, for example, `PROD-101` (BrewMaster Pro Espresso Machine, $120.00) and `PROD-404` (Heritage Pre-Seasoned Cast Iron Cocotte, $85.00).
1.3. `Electronics` are governed by `POL-ELC-002`. Final Sale and restricted items are governed by `POL-RES-004`. Where those policies apply, they take precedence over this document.

## 2. Return Window
2.1. The standard return window is **30 calendar days**.
2.2. The window starts on the **delivery date** for online orders, or the **purchase date** for in-store purchases.
2.3. Day 1 is the day after delivery or purchase. A return presented on Day 30 is within the window; a return presented on Day 31 or later must be declined.
2.4. Out-of-window returns that are clearly covered by this policy are declined in-store and do not require escalation to Operations (see `SOP-OPS-007`).

## 3. Item Condition Requirements
3.1. Items must be in **new or gently used condition**.
3.2. Items must be returned with their **original packaging and all included accessories** (manuals, parts, inserts, and attachments).
3.3. Items that are heavily used, soiled, altered, or missing major components are not eligible for return under this policy.

## 4. Returns With a Valid Receipt or Order ID
4.1. When the customer presents a valid receipt or a valid `order_id`, the associate issues a **full refund to the original payment method**.
4.2. Refunds are not converted to cash or to a different card when the original payment method was a card.
4.3. POS steps for the associate:
   1. Scan the receipt barcode or enter the `order_id` in the POS returns screen.
   2. Confirm the item is within the 30-day window (Section 2).
   3. Inspect the item condition and packaging (Section 3).
   4. Select "Return — Original Tender" and complete the refund.

## 5. Returns Without a Receipt
5.1. Without a receipt or `order_id`, the customer is eligible for **Store Credit only**.
5.2. No-receipt Store Credit is **capped at a maximum of $50.00** per return, regardless of the item's price.
5.3. The customer must present a **valid government-issued photo ID**, which is recorded at the POS.
5.4. Each customer is limited to **2 no-receipt returns per rolling 12-month period**. The POS will block a third attempt; associates must decline it.
5.5. Condition requirements in Section 3 still apply to no-receipt returns.

## 6. Examples
6.1. A customer returns an unopened `PROD-101` ($120.00) on Day 12 with a receipt: full $120.00 refund to the original card.
6.2. A customer returns a gently used `PROD-404` ($85.00) on Day 20 without a receipt and with a valid photo ID: $50.00 Store Credit (cap applies).
6.3. A customer returns `PROD-101` on Day 34 with a receipt: declined as outside the 30-day window.
