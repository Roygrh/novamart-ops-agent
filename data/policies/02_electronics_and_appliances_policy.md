# NovaMart Electronics and Appliances Policy

- **Policy ID:** POL-ELC-002
- **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15
- **Owner:** NovaMart Operations — Electronics Category Management
- **Summary:** Defines the 15-calendar-day return window for Electronics, the 15% open-box restocking fee for non-defective items, defective-item remedies, and post-window warranty referrals.

## 1. Scope
1.1. This policy applies to all products in the `Electronics` category, including:
   - `PROD-202` — NovaAir FPV Drone 4K ($450.00)
   - `PROD-303` — VisionMax 65" 4K OLED Smart TV ($1,200.00)
1.2. This policy overrides the 30-day window in `POL-RET-001` for all Electronics.
1.3. Mandatory inspection and handling steps for these products are listed in `PRD-CAT-006`.

## 2. Return Window
2.1. The Electronics return window is **15 calendar days** from the **delivery date** (online orders) or **purchase date** (in-store purchases).
2.2. A return presented on Day 15 is within the window; a return presented on Day 16 or later is outside the window.

## 3. Non-Defective Returns and the Open-Box Restocking Fee
3.1. If the package seal is **intact**, the item is refunded in full to the original payment method.
3.2. If the package seal is **broken** and the item is **NOT defective**, a **15% restocking fee** is deducted from the refund.
3.3. Restocking fee examples:
   - `PROD-202` ($450.00), opened, not defective: fee $67.50; refund $382.50.
   - `PROD-303` ($1,200.00), opened, not defective: fee $180.00; refund $1,020.00.
3.4. The restocking fee is never charged on defective items (Section 4).

## 4. Defective Electronics Within 15 Days
4.1. An item confirmed defective within the 15-day window qualifies for the customer's choice of:
   - a **free direct replacement** with the same `product_id`, or
   - a **100% refund** to the original payment method.
4.2. **No restocking fee** applies to defective items, even if the seal is broken.
4.3. POS steps for the associate:
   1. Enter the receipt or `order_id` and confirm the item is within 15 days.
   2. Complete the product-specific inspection in `PRD-CAT-006` (e.g., serial number check on `PROD-303`).
   3. Confirm and document the defect in the return notes.
   4. Select "Defective — Replacement" or "Defective — Full Refund" (restocking fee waived).

## 5. Returns After 15 Days
5.1. After 15 calendar days, in-store returns are **strictly declined**, whether or not the item is defective.
5.2. The associate directs the customer to the **1-year manufacturer warranty** and provides the manufacturer contact information printed in the product documentation.
5.3. Out-of-window denials clearly covered by this policy do not require escalation (see `SOP-OPS-007`).

## 6. Items Damaged in Transit
6.1. Electronics that arrive physically damaged from an online order are handled under `POL-ORD-003`.
6.2. Because `PROD-202` and `PROD-303` are both priced above $150.00, transit damage on these items always requires an Operations Escalation Ticket.
