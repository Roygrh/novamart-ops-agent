# NovaMart Price Match and Adjustments Policy

- **Policy ID:** POL-PRC-005
- **Effective Date:** 2026-01-15
- **Owner:** NovaMart Operations — Pricing & Merchandising
- **Summary:** Defines competitor price matching against four authorized retailers, the exclusions that disqualify a match, and the 14-day post-purchase price adjustment for NovaMart's own price reductions.

## 1. Scope
1.1. This policy applies to in-store and online purchases at NovaMart.
1.2. Price matches and adjustments are handled at the store register and are not escalated to Operations (see `SOP-OPS-007`).

## 2. Authorized Competitors
NovaMart matches prices **only** against these four authorized retailers:
2.1. **Amazon.com** — only when the item is **Sold and Shipped by Amazon.com**. Third-party marketplace sellers on Amazon are not eligible.
2.2. **Best Buy**.
2.3. **Target**.
2.4. **Walmart**.
Any other retailer is not eligible for a price match.

## 3. Competitor Price Match Requirements
3.1. The match is made against the competitor's price **at the time of purchase**.
3.2. The competitor item must have the **exact same `product_id`/model** as the NovaMart item.
3.3. The competitor item must be **currently in stock** at the competitor.
3.4. The associate verifies the competitor price on the competitor's official website or app at the register before applying the match.

## 4. Exclusions
Competitor price matches are **not** available for:
4.1. Marketplace third-party sellers (including non-Amazon.com sellers on Amazon).
4.2. Flash sales and limited-time lightning deals.
4.3. Black Friday and Cyber Monday promotions.
4.4. Bundle offers.
4.5. Clearance items, including Final Sale items such as `PROD-606` (see `POL-RES-004`).

## 5. Post-Purchase Price Adjustment
5.1. If **NovaMart lowers its own price** on an item within **14 calendar days** of purchase, the customer may request a **refund of the price difference**.
5.2. The adjustment can be requested at **any NovaMart store register** with a receipt or `order_id`.
5.3. The difference is refunded to the original payment method.
5.4. Requests made on Day 15 or later are declined.
5.5. POS steps for the associate:
   1. Enter the receipt or `order_id` and confirm the purchase date.
   2. Confirm the current NovaMart price of the same `product_id`.
   3. Select "Price Adjustment" and refund the difference.

## 6. Examples
6.1. A customer bought `PROD-101` at $120.00 and Target (in stock, same model) lists it at $110.00: match approved, $10.00 difference.
6.2. A customer shows `PROD-303` from a third-party marketplace seller on Amazon: declined.
6.3. NovaMart lowers `PROD-202` from $450.00 to $420.00 on Day 9 after purchase: $30.00 adjustment approved.
6.4. A customer requests a match on a Black Friday promotion: declined.
